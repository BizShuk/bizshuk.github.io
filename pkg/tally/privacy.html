Privacy Policy

On-device recognition and local inventory storage for a technical proof of concept.

1. Scope

This policy covers camera and LiDAR scanning, OCR and visual recognition, on-device Foundation Models, inventory records, scan assets and samples, categories and locations, and independent precise-location markers.

2. Information the App handles

The App may handle images, depth and point clouds, OCR text, visual embeddings, model output and diagnostics, inventory categories, balances and transactions, location labels, and precise coordinates for a marker. Markers are independent from inventory photos and records. It does not request contacts, microphone, health, payment, or advertising identifiers.

3. How information is used

Information is used for object recognition, result review, local inventory, sample comparison, and map markers. Processing uses device capabilities and supported Apple on-device models. It is not used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing and external services

Tally does not send inventory or recognition records to a developer backend. Apple Maps may request base-map resources under Apple's terms. Apple provides camera, LiDAR, location, MapKit, Vision, Core ML, and Foundation Models. A bundled recognition model has a separate license.

5. Storage and retention

Inventory, scan assets, recognition samples and embeddings, and markers are stored in the local SwiftData container. Large assets may use App-managed external local storage. Records remain until deleted in the App or the App is removed, subject to platform backup behavior.

6. Your privacy choices

You choose permissions, scans, accepted results, and markers. Location is requested When In Use for an explicit marker action. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Inventory items and markers can be deleted.

7. Security

Data uses the App's local container and iOS device security. Camera and precise-location records remain sensitive if the device, backup, or diagnostics are exposed. Never post private records publicly.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a technical proof of concept and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an App-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with features, models, data practices, or legal obligations. The effective date will be revised when published.

10. Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Do not post images, point clouds, precise coordinates, diagnostics, or inventory.
