# Tally Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026
Last updated August 10, 2026

Tally is an inventory-scanning proof of concept that processes recognition data
and stores inventory records on the device. It does not operate a developer
account or backend service.

## 1. Scope

This policy covers camera and LiDAR scanning, OCR and visual recognition,
on-device Foundation Models, inventory records, scan assets and samples,
user-created categories and locations, and independent precise-location map
markers.

## 2. Information the App handles

The App may handle camera images, depth and point-cloud data, OCR text, visual
embeddings, model output, recognition diagnostics, inventory categories,
balances and transactions, location labels, and precise geographic coordinates
when you create a map marker. A location marker is independent from inventory
photos and records. The App does not request contacts, microphone, health,
payment, or advertising identifiers.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used to recognize objects, let you review results, maintain a
local inventory, compare recognition samples, and display user-created markers.
Processing uses device capabilities and supported Apple on-device models. The
developer does not use information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or
sale.

## 4. Sharing and external services

Tally does not send inventory, images, point clouds, model output, or marker
coordinates to a developer backend. Apple Maps may request base-map resources
and process platform network information under Apple's terms. Apple provides
camera, LiDAR, location, MapKit, Vision, Core ML, and Foundation Models
capabilities. A bundled recognition model is subject to its separate license.

## 5. Storage and retention

Categories, balances, transactions, scan images, point clouds, recognition
samples and embeddings, and metadata markers are stored in the App's local
SwiftData container. Some large scan assets use external local storage managed
by the App. Records remain until you delete them through App controls or remove
the App, subject to device backups and platform behavior.

## 6. Your privacy choices

You choose whether to grant Camera and Location access, when to scan, which
recognized items to accept, and whether to create a location marker. Location
is requested When In Use for an explicit marker action. Permissions can be
changed in iOS Settings. Inventory items and markers can be deleted in the App,
and removing the App removes its local container.

## 7. Security

Data is kept in the App's local container and protected by iOS device security.
Precise location and camera-derived records can still be sensitive if the
device, backup, or exported diagnostic material is exposed. Keep the device
protected and do not place private records in public support issues.

## 8. Children's privacy

Tally is a technical proof of concept and is not directed to children under 13.
The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an
App-operated service.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when features, models, data practices, or legal
obligations change. The effective date will be revised when published.

## 10. Contact

Use the [public support issue tracker](https://github.com/bizshuk/bizshuk.github.io/issues)
for privacy and support requests. Do not post images, point clouds, precise
coordinates, model diagnostics, or personal inventory in a public issue.
