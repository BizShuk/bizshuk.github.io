Privacy Policy

Operator-selected camera streaming with no App-operated cloud relay.

1. Scope

This policy covers camera capture, optional audio, local-network listener discovery, manual endpoint entry, streaming controls, and device-local settings.

2. Information the App handles

The App handles live camera frames, optional microphone audio, listener addresses, RTMP paths, video configuration, and stream status. It does not save captured media and does not request contacts, photos, location, health, payment, or advertising identifiers.

3. How information is used

Camera and optional audio create the stream the operator starts. Endpoint and quality settings configure it and restore preferences. Information is not used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing and external services

The stream goes directly to the selected RTMP server, which may view, record, transform, redistribute, or retain it. The developer receives it only if an operator deliberately selects developer-operated infrastructure. Apple provides platform services, and the App includes open-source streaming components.

5. Storage and retention

Endpoint and operator preferences are stored locally. Captured video and audio are not persisted by the App. Downstream retention is controlled by the selected server owner.

6. Your privacy choices

You choose the listener, endpoint, microphone setting, and when to start or stop. Audio is off by default. Permissions may be revoked in iOS Settings. Downstream recordings must be managed with the server owner.

7. Security

Current RTMP transport has no encryption or endpoint authentication. Anyone with suitable network or listener access may observe the stream. Use only on a trusted, segmented network and not for sensitive capture.

8. Children's privacy

The App is an operator camera tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an App-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with features, transport, data practices, or legal obligations. The effective date will be revised when published.

10. Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Do not post stream keys, endpoints, recordings, or credentials.
