Privacy Policy

Local-network media transfer without a developer cloud relay.

1. Scope

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad source App and paired macOS receiver, including album selection, pairing, transfer, history, automatic sync, and optional deletion after sync.

2. Information the App handles

The App handles selected photo and video resources, album names, identifiers and metadata needed for export, receiver identity, pairing secrets, transfer status, destination bookmarks, and local logs. It does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location, health, payment, or advertising identifiers. iCloud-only media is skipped.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to pair devices, read selected media, transfer original resources, confirm delivery, resume work, and show local status. It is not used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing and external services

Media travels over TLS directly to the paired computer on the same local network. The developer does not receive it. Apple provides PhotoKit, Bonjour, Keychain, background scheduling, and platform services. Receiver software you choose may separately process destination files.

5. Storage and retention

The source stores configuration and pending state locally; pairing credentials use Keychain. The receiver stores a manifest, destination bookmark, completed media, and incomplete files in the chosen destination. Logs are bounded. Destination media remains until you delete it.

6. Your privacy choices

You choose albums, destination, receiver, automatic sync, and deletion settings. You may forget a pairing, reset the source, change destination, clear logs, remove the App, or delete destination files. Delete After Sync is off by default and requires system confirmation; library deletion may affect iCloud Photos devices.

7. Security

Pairing is explicit and transfer uses TLS 1.2 with a pre-shared secret. Protect both devices and your network. Receiver permissions and backups govern copied files after transfer.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a personal backup utility and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an App-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with App features, data practices, or legal obligations. The effective date will be revised when published.

10. Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Never post media, pairing secrets, identifiers, or private logs.
