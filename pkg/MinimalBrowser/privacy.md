# Minimal Browser Privacy Policy

Effective August 10, 2026
Last updated August 10, 2026

Minimal Browser is a macOS web browser. This policy explains the information
the App handles, where it goes, and the controls available to you.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to Minimal Browser for macOS, including grouped tabs,
website navigation, website storage, shared sign-in cookies, reading-state
restoration, page snapshots, and picture-in-picture preferences.

## 2. Information the App handles

The App handles URLs you enter, page titles, navigation and scroll state,
media-playback checkpoints, website cookies, local and session storage,
IndexedDB, service-worker caches, and temporary page snapshots. Websites may
receive your network address, browser requests, and any information you choose
to submit to them. Minimal Browser does not request contacts, photos, camera,
microphone, location, health, payment, or advertising identifiers.

## 3. How information is used

Information is used only to load the websites you choose, restore your browser
workspace, maintain website sign-in state, and provide the App's browsing and
media features. The developer does not use browsing information for analytics,
advertising, profiling, or sale.

## 4. Sharing and external services

Network requests travel directly from your Mac to the websites and services
you visit. Minimal Browser does not operate an intermediary browsing service.
DuckDuckGo may receive text entered as a search when an entry is not treated as
a URL. Each website applies its own privacy terms. Apple provides WebKit,
Keychain, and macOS platform services used by the App.

## 5. Storage and retention

Browser workspace state is stored in the App's local Application Support area.
Each tab has an isolated persistent website data store. Shared website cookies
may also be retained in the macOS Keychain. Page snapshots are temporary cache
files with a one-hour lifetime. Closing a tab removes that tab's isolated data
and snapshot; shared cookies remain until removed through website sign-out,
App controls, or removal of the App and its data.

## 6. Your privacy choices

You choose the websites you visit and information you provide. You may close a
tab or group to clear its isolated data, sign out on a website to remove its
session, clear relevant local data, or remove the App. Data already submitted
to a website must be managed through that website's controls.

## 7. Security

The App supports HTTPS and also permits user-selected local-network HTTP sites.
HTTP traffic is not encrypted. Shared cookies are stored using macOS Keychain,
but no storage or transmission method is completely secure. Review a site's
address and security before entering sensitive information.

## 8. Children's privacy

Minimal Browser is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to
children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's
personal information through an App-operated service.

## 9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change when App features, data practices, or legal obligations
change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of the policy.

## 10. Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the
[public support issue tracker](https://github.com/bizshuk/bizshuk.github.io/issues).
Do not place passwords, cookies, private browsing content, or other secrets in
a public issue.
