Privacy Policy

A direct browser connection, local state, and no developer-operated browsing backend.

1. Scope

This policy covers Minimal Browser for macOS, including grouped tabs, navigation, website storage, shared sign-in cookies, reading-state restoration, page snapshots, and picture-in-picture preferences.

2. Information the App handles

The App handles URLs, page titles, navigation and scroll state, media checkpoints, website cookies and storage, IndexedDB, service-worker caches, and temporary page snapshots. Websites may receive your network address, requests, and information you submit. The App does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, health, payment, or advertising identifiers.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to load chosen websites, restore your workspace, maintain website sign-in state, and provide browser features. The developer does not use browsing information for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing and external services

Requests travel directly to websites and services you choose. DuckDuckGo may receive search text when an entry is treated as a search. Each website applies its own privacy terms. Apple provides WebKit, Keychain, and macOS platform services.

5. Storage and retention

Workspace state is stored in local Application Support. Tabs use isolated persistent website stores. Shared cookies may remain in Keychain. Page snapshots are temporary cache files with a one-hour lifetime. Closing a tab removes its isolated data and snapshot; shared cookies remain until separately removed.

6. Your privacy choices

You may close tabs or groups, sign out from websites, clear relevant local data, or remove the App. Data submitted to a website must be managed through that website's controls.

7. Security

HTTPS is supported. User-selected local-network HTTP sites are also permitted and their traffic is not encrypted. Shared cookies use Keychain, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an App-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

The policy may change with App features, data practices, or legal obligations. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.

10. Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Do not post passwords, cookies, private browsing content, or secrets.
