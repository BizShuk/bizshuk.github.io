Privacy Policy

Device-local notification learning without a developer-operated backend.

1. Scope

This policy covers authorization, local examples, remote registration and callbacks, APNs token display and copying, notification actions, replies, and the local arrival journal.

2. Information the App handles

The App handles permission status, notification content and metadata, action selections, reply text, the APNs device token, and a local arrival journal. The full token appears only when explicitly copied. It does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, health, payment, or advertising identifiers.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to schedule and present notifications, process actions, display registration and delivery state, and maintain a bounded local journal. It is not used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or sale.

4. Sharing and external services

APNs assigns and transports device tokens and remote payloads under Apple's platform operation. The App does not send its token or journal to a developer server. A sender receives the token only when you copy and provide it.

5. Storage and retention

The latest token and bounded journal are stored in device-local settings. Notification Center may retain content until removed by you or the system. Independent sender records follow that sender's policy.

6. Your privacy choices

You choose authorization, local examples, token copying, replies, and senders. Permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. You may clear notifications and journal data where provided or remove the App.

7. Security

Treat the APNs token as sensitive routing information. The App masks it by default, but copied information moves outside App control. Never post tokens or private payloads publicly.

8. Children's privacy

The App is a developer utility and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect children's information through an App-operated service.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may change with App features, data practices, or legal obligations. The effective date will be revised when published.

10. Contact

Use the public support issue tracker. Never post tokens, payloads, replies, or credentials.
